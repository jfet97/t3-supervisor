Settlement now covers the complete participant set of a completed task: besides the main and its Supervisor, the main's lineage descendants and the local T3 conversations its contributions declared settle once per task by their own automatic-settle command on the update time their fresh read showed, so T3 rejects the command atomically when the conversation changed or its user chose in between; a conversation in a linked environment has no supported route and is listed unsupported without a command. What cannot settle is recorded and shown with its reason and read afresh on each pass, keeping its command and attempt count through unreadable or busy passes; a lost reply is retried after the quiet window, one command is sent at most three times, and each command is admitted only while the same task is still complete and the participant still a member. Settlement records belong to the task and revision they were evaluated on, so the next task on the same main evaluates a reused reviewer anew while the earlier rows stay as history; intermediate review rounds never settle a reviewer, and participants that are not T3 conversations are shown as not applicable. In History, each earlier review attempt is headed by its target and time instead of a return count that is not the occurrence's attempt.
