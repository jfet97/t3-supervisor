After a task completes, the service makes one best-effort automatic settle attempt per T3 participant besides the main and its Supervisor: the main's lineage descendants and the local T3 conversations its contributions declared are each evaluated once per completed execution, and one that is readable, quiet and not kept active by its user gets a single automatic-settle command on the update time its fresh read showed, which T3 rejects atomically when the conversation changed or its user chose in between. The outcome is recorded from native evidence (settled, superseded by its user, unknown after a lost reply, or rejected); anything else is recorded once with its reason, including conversations in a linked environment that have no supported route, and left to manual settlement, with nothing retried. The command is admitted only while the same execution is still complete and the participant still a member. An execution is the task, its scope revision and the main document's latest completion record, so the next task, or the same task completed again after a return, evaluates every participant anew while the earlier rows stay as history; the progress page shows the current execution's rows on any workflow page. Intermediate review rounds never settle a reviewer, and participants that are not T3 conversations are shown as not applicable. In History, each earlier review attempt is headed by its target and time instead of a return count that is not the occurrence's attempt.
